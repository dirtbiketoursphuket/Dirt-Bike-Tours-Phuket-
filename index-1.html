Phuket Viewpoints

Some of the routes take you to these classic lookouts

Promthep Cape

Southern tip of Phuket. Dramatic cliffs and wide Andaman Sea views — famous for sunset.

Karon Viewpoint

Classic three-beach panorama. Look down on Kata Noi, Kata and the long curve of Karon Beach.

Black Rock Viewpoint

Elevated jungle viewpoint overlooking Nai Harn and the southern coastline. Quieter and more adventurous.
